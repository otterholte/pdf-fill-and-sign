Why these are free

Every one of these tools runs entirely inside your browser. Your file is opened on your own device, changed on your own device, and saved back to your own device. Nothing is sent to a server, which means there is no server to pay for — so there is nothing to charge you for, no matter how many people use them.

I build software for a living. These are the honest version of a portfolio: real tools, doing real work, that you can use today without giving me an email address.
